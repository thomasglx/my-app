→ MyApp bookmark

On a LinkedIn profile, one click sends the page you are viewing to the person's contact card in MyApp. It only reads the page you have open: nothing runs on LinkedIn by itself.

  1. Show the bookmarks bar in Safari: View → Show Favorites Bar (Cmd+Shift+B).
  2. Drag this button onto the bar: → MyApp
  3. On a LinkedIn profile (linkedin.com/in/…), scroll down once so Experience loads, then click → MyApp.
